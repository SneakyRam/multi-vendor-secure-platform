# Participant Rules — Build Secure 24 Hrs Hackathon

**Abhedya — VBIT Cybersecurity Forum, Vignana Bharathi Institute of Technology, Hyderabad**

All participants must read and adhere to these rules throughout the 24-hour event.

---

## 1. Team Composition

- Each team consists of **either 2 or 4 registered participants** (teams of 1, 3, or greater than 4 are strictly prohibited).
- Team composition cannot be modified during the event.
- All team members share responsibility for the submitted work and must be recorded in `metadata/team.yaml`.

---

## 2. Live Project Creation & Origin Rules

- Teams **must start** from the official Abhedya starter repository.
- **Strict Live Authorship**: All application logic, APIs, and components must be **authored live during the official 24-hour hackathon window** (October 5, 2026, 11:00 AM IST to October 6, 2026, 11:00 AM IST).
- **Prohibition of Pre-Existing Repositories**:
  - Submitting, copying, cloning, or adapting pre-existing projects or open-source repositories as the project solution is **strictly prohibited** and results in immediate disqualification.
  - Standard framework scaffolding and standard public library dependencies via official package managers are allowed.

---

## 3. AI-Assisted Development & Automated Logging

- AI coding assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Cursor, etc.) are **fully permitted**.
- **Automated Logging**:
  - The AI agent is instructed via `AGENTS.md` to automatically log every prompt, the full agent response, exact file changes, and timeline into `docs/logs.txt`.
  - Prompts, agent responses, and approach logs must **not be forged, manipulated, or falsified**.
- Teams remain fully responsible for explaining and defending their implementation during evaluation.

---

## 4. GitHub & Development Practices

- Teams must create a new GitHub repository for their project and record its URL in `metadata/team.yaml`.
- Teams must maintain continuous development and commit/push regularly after each prompt turn.
- Every turn must record the Git commit SHA in `docs/logs.txt`.
- **Do not** force-push to erase development history.
- **Do not** access or copy from other teams' repositories.

---

## 5. Submission Evaluation

- Submissions will be evaluated based on the project implementation, architecture, and live deployment.

---

## 6. Submission Freeze

- **Deadline**: October 6, 2026, 11:00 AM IST (`2026-10-06T11:00:00+05:30`).
- The final submission will be evaluated from the frozen commit SHA recorded in `metadata/submission.yaml` at the deadline.
- Post-deadline commits (committed after October 6, 2026, 11:00 AM IST) will not be evaluated.

---

*By participating in Build Secure 24, all team members agree to abide by these rules.*
